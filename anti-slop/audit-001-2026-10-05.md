# antislop audit 001: site copy

Date: 2026-10-05
Skill: antislop-copywriting (read with antislop core)
Mode: AFTER (audit). Nothing is changed until you approve specific numbers.
Scope: every rendered string: header, hero, practice areas, attorneys, FAQ,
contact form, footer, "Your brief" cart, preview badge.

Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM,
Quality Locks = LOW.

## Findings

### F-01 — HIGH — R-17 (numbers without a source), R-36
`src/data/content.js:138` ("Selected experience", Haziq Azhari)
> "Drafted and advised on 1,041 wasiat and hibah instruments..."

A precise count shown as fact with no source attached. The rule says empty is
better than unsourced. Confirm the figure with the firm, or remove the number
and describe the work without it.

### F-02 — MEDIUM — R-06 (uppercase label as default, purpose required)
`src/styles/globals.css` `.eyebrow` and `.people__role`, applied to: hero
"Advocates & Solicitors · Kuala Lumpur", practice "Our practice", people
"The team", FAQ "Good questions", contact "Consultation", cart "Anything
specific?", plus "Managing Partner" / "Partner" tags.

Uppercase plus wide tracking is the default label treatment on every section.
That can be a deliberate label system, but the rule asks for the reason to be
written down. Either state the reason, or vary at least one label so it reads
as a choice.

### F-03 — LOW — R-16 (empty AI vocabulary)
`src/components/Partners.jsx:58`
> "Legal journey"

"Journey" is on the empty-vocabulary list. Rename to "Background" or "Career".

### F-04 — LOW — R-16, C-1 (vague boilerplate)
`src/data/content.js:143` and `src/data/content.js:187`
> "Committed to bridging Islamic legal principles..." / "Committed to
> practical, commercially minded legal solutions..."

Both partner aspirations open with the same "Committed to" and end on vague
phrases ("strategic legal services", "practical, commercially minded legal
solutions"). Replace each with one concrete commitment, or cut the
"Aspiration" block entirely.

### F-05 — LOW — R-36 (rhythm tell: forced triad)
`src/components/Hero.jsx:29`
> "Plainly, deliberately, and with your long view in mind."

An adverb triad. Single instance and it has voice, so optional. If trimmed:
"Plainly, and with your long view in mind."

### F-06 — LOW — R-31, C-1 (naming collision)
`src/components/Contact.jsx:211` (and the mailto body at `:43`)
> "Brief description"

The site now uses "brief" as the cart noun ("Your brief"). "Brief description"
reads as a second, different brief. Rename to "Short description" or "What is
this about?".

## Checks that passed (evidence)

- R-02 em dash: zero `—` characters in `src` (rg over jsx/js/css returned 0).
- R-15 CTAs: "Book a consultation", "See the practice areas", "Request a
  consultation", "Send my brief", "Add to brief" are action-specific; no
  Get Started / Learn More / Explore.
- R-16 buzzwords: no seamless / revolutionary / cutting edge / next
  generation / AI-powered in rendered copy.
- R-17 (apart from F-01): no other statistics or counts.
- R-28 FAQ: all seven questions are firm-specific (partner-led, conflict
  checks, bringing documents, personal vs business).
- R-36/R-38: no fabricated testimonials, logos, or trust claims; the preview
  badge labels itself as a preview; phone, email, address are real.
- Hygiene: no emoji headings, no mechanical boldface, no scare-quote clusters,
  no chatbot closers, no signposting, no all-caps prose emphasis.
