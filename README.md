# Hazicolaw

The website of Haziq Azhari & Co., an advocates and solicitors practice in Solaris Dutamas, Kuala Lumpur.

It introduces the firm's practice areas and the partners who run it, answers the questions clients tend to ask before a first meeting, and gives visitors a way to start an enquiry.

## Home

The firm's name, designation, and city, with a short statement of how it works and two ways forward: book a consultation, or go straight to the practice areas.

## Frequently asked questions

Seven questions, covering how quickly a consultation can be arranged, who the visitor will speak with, what to bring to the first conversation, whether the firm advises on both personal and business matters, disputes and litigation, inheritance matters, and how to tell whether the firm is the right fit.

## Contact and enquiries

The contact section carries an enquiry form and the firm's own details. The form asks for a name, email address, phone number, the closest matter type, and a short description, together with an acknowledgment that sending it does not create a solicitor-client relationship.

A visitor can start an enquiry in three ways: the form, email, or either partner's direct line. Submitting the form either sends the enquiry through the firm's form endpoint or opens it as a draft in the visitor's own mail client, depending on how the site is configured.
